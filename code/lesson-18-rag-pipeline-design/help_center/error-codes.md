# Error codes

## PAY-4012

Your bank declined the card. The usual reasons are a daily spending limit or online payments being switched off on that card. Turn on online payments in your bank's app or try another card. We can't see the exact reason, because the bank doesn't share it with us.

## PAY-4021

The card's expiry date or CVV didn't match what the bank has on record. Check both numbers and enter them again. After three wrong tries the bank may block online payments on the card for 24 hours.

## PAY-4027

The UPI request timed out before you approved it in your UPI app. You should wait 30 minutes before you try again, so the first request can expire. If you approve the old request and start a new one too, you could pay twice.

## PAY-4072

The payment went through 3D Secure, but the one-time password you typed was wrong or had expired. Ask for a fresh password with the Resend button and enter it within five minutes.

## PAY-4120

Your net banking session closed before the payment finished, usually because the bank's page was left open too long. Start the payment again and finish it in one go without switching apps.

## PAY-5003

Cash on delivery is not available at your pin code. The courier that serves your area doesn't collect cash. Choose UPI, a card or net banking to place the order.

## PAY-5030

The order total is above the cash on delivery limit. Remove an item to bring the total down, or pay online.

## DEL-2104

The courier couldn't reach you on the phone number saved with the order. Update the number from the tracking link and the next attempt will use the new one.

## DEL-2140

The address was incomplete, most often because the flat number or a landmark was missing. Add the missing details from the tracking link before the next attempt.

## DEL-2410

The package was damaged on its way to you and has been sent back to our warehouse. We ship a fresh one automatically and you don't need to do anything.

## ACC-1007

The one-time code you entered at sign-in was wrong. Request a new code and use the latest one, because every new code cancels the one before it.

## ACC-1070

Your account is locked for one hour after five failed sign-in attempts in a row. Wait for the hour to pass and then sign in again. Resetting the password doesn't remove the lock.

## RET-3301

The return window for this item has closed. Refunds are possible for a limited time after delivery, and this order is past that limit. A warranty claim may still be open to you.

## RET-3310

A return request already exists for this item, so a second one can't be opened. Open the order to see the status of the first request and its pickup date.
