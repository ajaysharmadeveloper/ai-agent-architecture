# Product care

## Charging your headphones

The AX-220 and the AX-220 Pro charge over USB-C. A full charge takes about 2 hours and gives up to 30 hours of playback on the AX-220, and up to 24 hours on the AX-220 Pro with noise cancelling on. Use a charger rated at 5 volts. The light on the right ear cup turns from red to white when the battery is full.

## Cleaning running shoes

Don't put running shoes in a washing machine, because the heat and the spin weaken the glue in the sole. Take out the insoles and laces, brush off dry mud, and wipe the upper with a cloth and mild soap. Let the shoes dry in the shade. Direct sun and hair dryers make the midsole foam hard.

## Looking after a water bottle

Wash the bottle by hand with warm water before the first use. The steel body is fine in a dishwasher, but the lid is not, because the heat damages the seal that keeps it from leaking. Don't put fizzy drinks in the bottle, and don't put it in a freezer.
