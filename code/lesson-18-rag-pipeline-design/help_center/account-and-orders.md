# Account and orders

## Changing your phone number

To change the phone number on your account, go to Profile and tap Edit phone. We send a one-time code to the old number first and then to the new one. If you have lost access to the old number, contact support with a photo ID and we will update it by hand within 2 working days.

## Downloading an invoice

A GST invoice is ready within 24 hours of delivery. Open the order and tap Download invoice. If you need a company name and GSTIN on it, add them at checkout, because we can't change an invoice after it has been issued.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. You can use one across several orders until the balance runs out. A gift card can't be returned, refunded or exchanged for cash. If you return an order that was paid with a gift card, the amount goes back to the same gift card.

## Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account from Profile, under Privacy. The account is switched off at once and deleted for good after 30 days. During those 30 days you can sign in again to stop the deletion. Invoices are kept for as long as tax law requires, even after the account is gone.
