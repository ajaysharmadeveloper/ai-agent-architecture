# Payments

## Ways to pay

You can pay by UPI, credit card, debit card or net banking. Cash on delivery is available on orders up to ₹5,000 at most pin codes. Above that amount the cash option is hidden at checkout, and you will need to pay online. We don't store your card number. Saved cards are held by the payment gateway as tokens.

## Payment failed but money was deducted

If your bank took the money and we didn't confirm the order, you don't need to do anything. The amount is reversed by your bank automatically in 3 to 5 working days. Please don't pay a second time for the same cart until you have checked your Orders page, because the first payment sometimes confirms a few minutes late.

## Payment error codes

When a payment fails, checkout shows an error code that starts with PAY. Each code has its own cause and its own fix, and the Error codes page lists all of them. Note the code down before you close the page, because support will ask for it.

## EMI

EMI is available on orders above ₹3,000 with selected credit cards. The checkout page lists the banks and the monthly amount before you confirm. If you return an order bought on EMI, we refund the full amount to the card, and your bank cancels the remaining instalments within one billing cycle.
