# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times

Most orders leave our warehouse within one working day. After that, delivery to metro cities takes 2 to 4 working days, and delivery to every other pin code takes 5 to 8 working days. Large items such as luggage can take two days longer. The date shown on your Orders page is our best estimate and it updates each time the package is scanned.

## Shipping fee

Shipping is free on orders above ₹499. For anything below that, we add a flat shipping fee of ₹49 at checkout, whatever the weight and wherever it is going. The fee is shown before you pay. If you return the whole order, the shipping fee is refunded along with the item price.

## Tracking your package

Every shipped order gets a tracking link by SMS and email. The Orders page shows the same information: where the package was last scanned and which hub it is moving to next. Tracking can look stuck for up to 24 hours while a package travels between two cities, and that is normal.

## Marked delivered but not received

Sometimes tracking says delivered and you have nothing in your hands. First check with your neighbours, your building security and anyone else at home, because couriers often leave a package with them. If it still hasn't turned up, report it to us within 48 hours of the delivered scan. We open an investigation with the courier, which takes up to 3 working days, and then we either find the package or send you a replacement.

## Delivery attempts

The courier makes three delivery attempts on three separate working days. You get an SMS before each one. If all three fail, the package is returned to our warehouse, and a prepaid order is refunded automatically once it arrives there. You can ask for a different time slot from the tracking link after the first failed attempt.

## Changing the delivery address

You can change the address only until the package leaves our warehouse. Open the order, tap Edit address and save the new one. Once the status says shipped, the address is locked, because the label has already been printed. In that case you can refuse the delivery and place a new order to the right address.
