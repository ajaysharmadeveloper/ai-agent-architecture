# Returns and refunds

## Who can get a refund

A delivered order can be refunded in full within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days start on the day the courier marks the order as delivered, not on the day you placed it. You don't need to give a reason, but the item has to come back in the condition it arrived in, with its box and any accessories. We allow one refund per order, so if an order has two items, ask for both in the same request.

## Orders that are still on the way

A shipped order can't be refunded until it is delivered. If you no longer want it, you have two choices. You can refuse the package at the door, and it comes back to us by itself. Or you can accept it and then start a return from the Orders page. In both cases the refund starts only after the package is scanned back at our warehouse.

## How long the money takes

For orders paid online, the money goes back to the original payment method in 5 to 7 working days. That means the same card, the same UPI ID or the same bank account you paid from. We can't send it anywhere else. Your bank may take one more day to show it on your statement, and weekends and bank holidays don't count as working days.

## Cash on delivery orders

A cash on delivery order has no original payment method to send the money back to, so the refund works differently. After the return is picked up, we send you a secure link by SMS. You enter your bank account number and IFSC code there, and we pay the refund by bank transfer. It reaches you 7 to 10 working days after you submit the details. We never ask for bank details on a call or over chat.

## Return pickup

Once a return is approved, our courier partner collects the item from your address within 3 working days. Keep the item packed and hand it over with the original box. The courier gives you a pickup receipt, and your refund clock starts from that scan. If the pickup fails twice because nobody was home, the return request is closed and you will need to raise a new one.

## Items that can't be returned

A few items are final sale for hygiene or safety reasons. In-ear headphones and earbuds can't be returned once the seal is broken, because they sit inside the ear. Innerwear, socks and personal care products follow the same rule. Gift cards can't be returned or refunded at all. If one of these items arrives damaged or faulty, it is covered under the warranty or the damaged-on-arrival process instead.
