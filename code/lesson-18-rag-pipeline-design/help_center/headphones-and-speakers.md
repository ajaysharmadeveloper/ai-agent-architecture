# Headphones and speakers

## AX-200

The AX-200 is our wired on-ear headphone. It has a 1.2 metre cable with a 3.5 mm plug and no battery, so there is nothing to charge. It weighs 140 grams and folds flat for travel.

## AX-210

The AX-210 is the wired model for calls. It has an inline microphone and a mute switch on the cable, and it plugs in over USB-C instead of a 3.5 mm jack. It weighs 150 grams.

## AX-220

The AX-220 is the everyday wireless model. It connects over Bluetooth 5.3, pairs with two devices at once and weighs 190 grams. There is no noise cancelling on this model.

## AX-220 Pro

The AX-220 Pro adds active noise cancelling and a transparency mode to the AX-220. It weighs 210 grams and comes with a hard carry case and an audio cable for flights.

## AX-250

The AX-250 is the wireless sports model. It has ear hooks, an IPX5 rating for sweat and rain, and a battery that lasts up to 18 hours. It weighs 28 grams and is not made for swimming.

## AX-300

The AX-300 is the over-ear studio model. It has 50 mm drivers, replaceable ear pads and a battery that lasts up to 45 hours. It weighs 310 grams, which makes it the heaviest headphone we sell.

## BX-100

The BX-100 is a pocket Bluetooth speaker with a 5 watt driver. It plays for up to 10 hours and has a loop for hanging it from a bag. It is not water resistant.

## BX-120

The BX-120 is the outdoor speaker. It is rated IPX7, so it survives being dropped in water for a short time, and it floats. It has a 12 watt driver and plays for up to 16 hours.

## BX-150

The BX-150 is the party speaker. It has a 30 watt driver, a carry handle and lights that follow the music. It plays for up to 24 hours and can charge a phone from its USB port.
