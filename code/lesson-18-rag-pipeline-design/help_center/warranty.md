# Warranty

## Headphones

Our headphones come in three models, and each one has its own warranty period. The AX-200 is the wired model and it is covered for 6 months. The AX-220 is the wireless model and it is covered for 12 months. The AX-220 Pro, the wireless model with noise cancelling, is covered for 18 months. The period starts on the delivery date. The warranty covers faults in the drivers, the battery and the charging port.

## Running shoes

Running shoes are covered for 90 days against manufacturing defects. That means things like the sole separating from the upper, stitching that comes apart or an eyelet that pulls out. Normal wear on the outsole is not a defect. Send us a photo of the fault and of the size label inside the shoe, and we will replace the pair or refund it.

## Water bottles

Water bottles don't carry a long warranty, but we do replace a bottle that leaks. Report the leak within 7 days of delivery with a short video that shows where the water comes out. We send a new bottle and you don't need to return the old one. Dents and scratches from use are not covered.

## What voids the warranty

The warranty ends early if the product was opened or repaired by anyone other than our service partner. It also doesn't cover water damage on models that are not rated for it, damage from a fall, or a battery that was charged with a charger above the rated voltage. Cosmetic marks don't affect the warranty as long as the product works.

## How to make a claim

To claim, open the order and choose Warranty claim. Upload the invoice, which you can download from the same page, and a photo or video of the fault. Our service partner replies within 2 working days. If the product has to be sent in, we arrange the pickup and it costs you nothing.
